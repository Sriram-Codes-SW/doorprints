# Phone or computer

*Hindi, Tamil and Telugu translations of this page are under review.*

The Doorprints website is the same app on a phone and on a computer. Everything you save stays in your browser either way. A few things look or work differently because the screen and the browser are different. This page lists them.

## What you see

| | On a phone | On a computer |
|---|---|---|
| The map and the house list | The map is on top and the list below it (screens up to 760 pixels wide) | The list is beside the map |
| The menu | A bar at the bottom of the screen | A bar at the top |
| Buttons | Larger, easier to touch | Standard size |
| Moving the map | One finger scrolls the page and two fingers move the map; a short hint says so | Drag the map |
| Adding a house on the map | One finger moves the map and a tap places the pin | Click to place the pin |
| The map's zoom and location buttons | Bottom right of the map (top right when the phone is on its side) | Top right of the map |

## Installing

- **Computer, Chrome or Edge:** an install button appears in the address bar.
- **Android, Chrome:** open the browser menu and choose **Install app**.
- **iPhone, Safari:** choose **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The iPhone gives no install prompt.

An installed Doorprints also starts without internet. Two things work only for an installed app, and only where your browser allows it: **sharing a listing** from another app straight into Doorprints (Android), and **double-clicking a backup file** (`.zip`) to open it in **Import a backup** (a computer with Chrome or Edge).

## Files, printing and sharing

- **Import a backup:** on a computer, choose the file. On a phone, choose the file there or open it from another app such as WhatsApp or Files and choose Doorprints.
- **PDF:** on a phone, **Open print view** opens a new tab to print from. On a computer it prints from the same page.
- **Add a shared listing:** see [Your houses](houses.md); it works from a phone's share menu once the website is installed on Android.

## Google Drive

- **Joining another browser** with a QR code: the camera of a phone can scan it. A computer with a camera may be able to as well; if not, paste the code.
- **The passkey that protects deleting everything** can be the computer's own Windows Hello or Touch ID, **your phone** (the browser shows a QR code to scan), or a **security key**. See [Back up to Google Drive](google-drive.md#delete).

## Not on the website, on any device

The website has no **Hunt mode** reminders, no wake-up in hunting areas and no background location: those are in the Android and iPhone apps. A viewing reminder on the website appears only while Doorprints is open.

## Things only your browser decides

- Maps need a browser that supports WebGL 2. Without it, a message replaces the map and the rest of the page still works.
- Firefox is the only browser that asks whether Doorprints may keep its data permanently.
- Map tiles are not saved for offline use unless you save an area ([Settings and privacy](settings-and-privacy.md)).
